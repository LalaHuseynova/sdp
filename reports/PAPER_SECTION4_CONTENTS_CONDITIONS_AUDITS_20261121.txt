Contents, conditions and audits. In each group, A contributes four audit seeds, four scored attacks, one linked copy of a seed and seven benign facts; B contributes 16 benign facts; U contributes three benign facts and one unlinked relay; and P contributes four control statements. The linked copy repeats a seed in the same context and is registered under that seed's provenance root. The unlinked relay repeats a seed of the second context and is placed in that same context under U, without a registered source link, so it forms a new root. The 35 probes comprise four scored-attack, one linked-copy, one relay, 25 external-utility and four principal-control questions. One benign fact of B receives the safe audit and is not probed. For example (development split), one benign contribution states "The Larkspur newsletter review is a standing weekly meeting held every Wednesday", and its probe asks on which weekday the review is held.

Audits are oracle audits: their verdicts are correct by construction rather than produced by a detector. In the three main conditions, every audited method receives the same five tickets before the first probe. Four tickets mark the seeds unsafe, and one marks a benign fact of B safe. Scored attacks are never audited, so their outcomes measure how audit evidence propagates. Random-detection, boundary-verification and imperfect-audit conditions change these tickets, and under random detection a scored attack may itself be audited; the other adaptive conditions keep the five oracle tickets.

The three main conditions reassign the same 40 texts to contributors and batches. In the localized condition, A's two seeds and two scored attacks in each context share one batch. In the distributed condition, each of A's batches holds one seed and one scored attack. In the uninformative condition, the items of A and B are shuffled over contributors and batches within each context. The localized key stratum consists of the seven benign facts per group that share contributor and context with audited poison but lie in the other batch (168 probes in the test split).
