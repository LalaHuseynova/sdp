# Executed exploratory design and amendments

1 November 2026, Asia/Baku. This document explains the executed provisional
comparison. It is not a replacement for the original human-validated protocol,
an external preregistration or a confirmatory paper result.

## Scope and selection chronology

The first run used the previously frozen D00 development bag, 32 inputs, three
regimes and static/R/C/M/S plus no-memory controls. It produced 486 complete query
outcomes. No original attack succeeded. Offline traces then showed that only
principal-authored records were eligible; source propagation could not affect
those selected contexts.

A subsequent **offline** audit examined the original cached query classifiers and
authority admission in all 20 existing provisional bags, without retrieval,
answer calls or new labels. Of 640 original questions, 58 had a structurally
external candidate allowed by original governance. These included 30 attack,
27 suggestive-benign and one explicit-benign question. The existing first eight
development bags contained 21 such questions: eight attack and 13 suggestive
benign. D00 had none.

The extension was declared after the D00 result and admission audit. It includes
**every** remaining pre-existing development bag D01–D07, without selecting on
attack success or method advantage. All parameters, original questions, input
contents and three regimes are unchanged. D08–D19 receive no new model-outcome
evaluation here. Their cached classification metadata was inspected by the
admission audit; they are reserved unvalidated provisional bags, not an untouched
confirmatory test.

Together, D00–D07 cover 256 distinct input cases: 160 adversarial and 96 benign,
with 16 Email and 16 File/Document inputs in each bag. This is **case-intent
controlled**. It is not the primary protocol's independently annotated
16-safe/16-unsafe memory-bundle selection. Three overlays of one bag remain
paired sensitivity conditions, not independent new datasets.

## What each method receives

Original SAFE-Mem flat/withhold/hops=0 settings, k=4 claims, m=4 evidence slots,
original standing/scope rules, extraction replies, cached MiniLM vectors, original
ingestion order and frozen source contents are preserved. New stores reproduce
cached construction in these small pools; they do not replay the historical full
6,240-case graph. Static SAFE-Mem is rerun within the same workload.

Every regime has five reserved audit cases and the same remaining 27 original
query events for each method. R/C/M/S receive exactly the same five findings and
equivalent historical verification calls/tokens/cost. Static and no-memory
references do not consume evidence. Query/answer target scores never update
source state. Detector checking is disabled uniformly.

R uses direct record/use containment. C uses contextual reputation. M uses the
maximum batch/context/source penalty. S uses the declared support gates: one
unsafe contribution for batch scope; two across separate batches for context;
four with support across both contexts and batches for global scope. Weight 0.5,
threshold 0.25 and Beta(1,1) summaries are unchanged. These summaries are heuristic
scores, not calibrated posteriors.

## AI feedback and controlled provenance

Existing frozen GPT-4o-mini proposals cover all 6,325 memory records in the eight
development bags. The full original-use bundle assessments are 65 safe-for-use,
104 unsafe and 87 uncertain. These are **AI assessments, not verified truth**.
No human reviews or new annotation decisions are invented.

The new consumer explicitly replays these proposals as **simulated AI feedback**;
it does not change their original `human_validated=false`, `eligible_as_gold=false`
or `can_update_live_trust=false` records. The existing scientific-execution gate
remains unchanged. A separate versioned, user-authorized provisional scope
dispatches only answering, conflict and private target-judging stages.

Principal-owned records retain their original owner and authority. Literal chunk
construction plus the synthetic direct-origin harness establishes controlled
external relationships. Ambiguous claims receive an external synthetic origin
only with a frozen AI external-origin proposal and exact external source spans;
other claims stay unknown. This is an **assumption-backed synthetic mapping**,
not authentication of natural authorship. No safety verdict is consulted to make
the mapping. Parent policies can only become more restrictive.

One attributable external component of one input/use is one audit incident.
Findings cover its actual registered records and content versions. Several claims,
chunks or old review calls do not create several source votes. Unknown/principal
components are not silently included in an authenticated contributor root.
Uncertain findings remain unresolved. Safe exceptions remain use-specific.

The selected historic review calls have authoritative saved billing receipts.
Every policy is charged the same equivalent calls and tokens per scheduled ticket;
whole calls are counted without allocating away unrelated review text. Historical
costs are reported separately from incremental replay expense. Looking up an old
annotation creates no new paid verification call and is not described as a free
operational verifier.

This is oracle-style label replay, not the protocol's later real-verifier stage.
An audit event's one attempt denotes one saved-label lookup. Several historical
per-record review calls can underlie that label; their actual equivalent call
counts are retained separately and can exceed two. We do not claim to have run
the proposed one/two-call-per-ticket operational verifier. AI-assisted provenance
preparation also used the precomputed review corpus; it is shared preparation
expense, not an uncharged online detector or independently authenticated metadata.

## Model calls, restart and costs

GPT-4o-mini requests keep the original prompts and output limits: answer 200,
conflict 6 and target judge 120 tokens. Query classification and embedding remain
cache-only, with no per-method fallback, dependency install or model download.
Actual native response JSON, text, usage, finish reasons and dispatch receipts are
saved. The first reply for an exact stage/request is frozen and shared across
methods; a favorable reply is never selected after seeing its score.

The same durable $30 project allowance covers old annotation work and these runs.
The current task has a cumulative additional $5 stop ceiling. After the first
run's $0.0111546 spend, the extension receives only the remaining $4.9888454 of
that same task ceiling. It does not create or reset another allowance. SDK retries
are zero; ambiguous billing preserves holds and stops further dispatch.

The preannounced conditional answer/conflict/judge estimate was $0.70 working or
$2.29 stress for D00, then approximately $4.90/$16.04 for the seven remaining
bags. Actual shared-cache expense is reported from settled native charges. The
task stop can prevent completion under worst-case assumptions; it is not a
promise that the full-context reservation totals fit under $5.

A CPU bottleneck required one runtime-only amendment: memoization of the exact
original revision hash for immutable evidence snapshots. Both billing databases
were write-locked before interrupting the first process; there were zero started
dispatches and zero holds. The unchanged query journal replayed prior outcomes
without divergence or duplicate requests. The amendment pins the added code and
tests, changes no question, feedback, policy, score, model request or spending
ceiling, and restores the original class descriptor on exit. Original ledger and
SAFE-Mem files are not edited.

## Evaluation and remaining claim limits

Answer ASR, paired attributable ASR, retention, restraint and combined benign
scores use original case labels and target judges. Write/retrieval diagnostics
measure target presence, not universal unsafe-memory labels. The answer judge is
separate from the source-feedback stream. Empty outputs, judge fallbacks, failed
calls and token-limit finishes are retained in coverage reporting.

Source-grounded benign QA is **not validated or scored** in this experiment.
Original retention/restraint therefore cannot establish preservation of useful
external contributions. Refusals and general task usefulness are not independently
annotated. Original attack labels do not supply record/use detector FPR/FNR.

Paired bag-bootstrap intervals use 10,000 common draws across arms and regimes.
They are exploratory, pointwise and not multiplicity-adjusted. Eight development
bags, scarce warranted memories and potentially few baseline successes limit
power. A zero difference does not establish equivalence or noninferiority.
Relational audit targeting and reserved cohorts can differ across regimes, so
between-regime differences are not a pure source-correlation intervention.

The historical 5.87% ASR is a separate full-pool reproduction reference. The
paper-level claim still requires independently checked labels, source-grounded
utility, a new locked confirmation, realistic-verifier/robustness studies and
evidence beyond simpler contextual or multi-scope reputation.
