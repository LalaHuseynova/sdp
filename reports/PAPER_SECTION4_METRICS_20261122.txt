Metrics. Every metric is either answer-level, computed from the model's answer, or retrieval-level, computed from the supplied context; the two levels are never combined. Judge ASR is the fraction of scored-attack probes for which SAFE-Mem's original judge (GPT-4o-mini, temperature 0) decides that the answer acts on the planted instruction rather than merely mentioning it. Target-specific ASR additionally requires the answer to restate the probed target, i.e., to contain at least half of the target's normalized content words; this is a lexical filter, not a human validation. Strict utility is the fraction of utility probes whose answer contains an alias of the inserted fact, under Unicode and case normalization and whole-token matching, and whose supplied context contains that fact. Key-stratum strict utility restricts strict utility to the localized key stratum, and innocent strict utility restricts it to the facts of B.

Retrieval exposure asks whether the supplied context contains a record from the probed contribution that restates the target or contains an alias of the fact. Unaudited exposure applies this test to scored attacks that are not themselves audited, useful-fact exposure applies it to utility probes, and relay exposure applies it to the unlinked relay. Linked-copy and principal-control probes are asked but enter no metric. Each estimate is computed per group first, as the rate over the group's eligible probes, with random-detection draws averaged within the group and several cells weighted equally, and is then averaged over the 24 groups.
