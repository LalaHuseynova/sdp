Methods and settings. We compare eleven methods on the same stores. Original SAFE-Mem (static) receives no audits. R withholds a record only for the question under which an audit marked it unsafe. C, M and S also penalize other records from authenticated external contributors. For each provenance scope, they estimate the scope's risk from its audit outcomes under a Beta(1, 1) prior, counting each audited root once. The penalty is the amount by which this risk exceeds 1/2. A penalized record is ranked lower by 0.5 times its penalty, and it is withheld once the penalty reaches 0.25, which takes two unsafe outcomes when the scope has no safe outcome. C uses the contributor's scope in the record's context, and M uses the maximum over batch, context and contributor. S uses only the scopes its evidence gates activate: the batch after one unsafe outcome in that batch, the context after two unsafe outcomes in two different batches, and the contributor after at least four unsafe outcomes in total, with two contexts that each meet the context gate.

SL adds content lineage to S: a claim whose embedding has cosine similarity of at least 0.8 to a claim of an unsafe-audited root is withheld. S2-ID adds MemLineage's sensitive-action gate to SL: if a question is classified as sensitive and the context contains an externally derived record, the context is emptied. Four published defenses serve as external baselines: A-MemGuard, MemLineage, an LLM sanitizer and RandEC. They filter the records that original SAFE-Mem retrieves, and they cannot use audit verdicts or contributor identity. Withholding removes a record from the answering context. In R, C, M, S, SL and S2-ID, the freed slot is refilled with the next eligible claim, whereas the published defenses filter without refill. All methods share construction, prompts, retrieval settings, answer model and judge.

GPT-4o-mini (via OpenRouter) performs retention, extraction, query classification, conflict checks, answering and attack judging at temperature 0, and it also serves the language-model calls of the published defenses. The local all-MiniLM-L6-v2 encoder (Reimers & Gurevych, 2019) provides embeddings; neither model is fine-tuned. Answers are limited to 200 tokens, and each answering context holds up to four claims. SAFE-Mem's optional raw-chunk external-evidence mechanism is disabled, so external content reaches the answer model only through extracted claims, which remain admissible. A-MemGuard uses its repository setting of temperature 1.0 with one draw, and RandEC uses a local DistilBERT classifier. As a second backbone, Claude Haiku 5.5 replaces GPT-4o-mini as the answer model for the eleven methods in eight cells, with the same prompt and token limit and with reasoning disabled. Its endpoints accept no temperature setting, so it samples at the model's default temperature, and GPT-4o-mini remains the judge. The constants of R, C, M and S (prior, ranking weight, threshold and gates) were fixed in source files hashed before the v4 test split was first evaluated and were not changed afterwards. SL and S2-ID, including the lineage threshold, were designed on development data after the first test results were known.
