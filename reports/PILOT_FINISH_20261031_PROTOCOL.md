## Data and label boundaries

Runtime/model inputs exclude original input labels, expected targets, risk profiles,
method outcomes, earlier review votes and future audits. Source text is untrusted
data. JSON-schema restrictions improve response contracts; they do not establish
semantic safety, author identity, calibration or reviewer accuracy.

All record/use labels are AI proposals (Z-hat), distinct from human Z and original
case intent Y_case. Human-verified label/question counts stay zero. Incomplete and
uncertain bundles cannot fill safe quotas. A safe input needs external stored-claim
support and a separately AI-accepted benign question for exploratory eligibility.
ACL permissions and original authority remain separate from empirical risk.
