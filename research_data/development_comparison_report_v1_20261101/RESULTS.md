# Eight-group provisional dynamic-defense results

**Real model outputs, AI-simulated feedback, eight existing development groups (256 inputs). Human validation pending.**

D00 was an initial run with no eligible external records. The extension included every remaining pre-existing development bag D01-D07, with unchanged methods and parameters. Case selection did not depend on outcomes. These are exploratory development results, not a locked confirmatory test.

The bags retain the earlier provisional composition: 16 Email and 16 File/Document inputs, 20 attack cases and 12 benign cases each. They were not selected to satisfy the protocol's independently verified 16-unsafe/16-safe bundle quota. Regime names describe the existing case-label metadata construction; they do not establish actual memory-risk correlation.

## Limits and interpretation

Three overlays of a bag are paired sensitivity conditions, not independent new datasets. Pointwise paired bag-bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples) and all S-versus-baseline contrasts are in comparison.json; they are exploratory and not multiplicity-adjusted. No equivalence or utility noninferiority is established by a null point estimate.

Feedback is replayed from saved AI proposals, not verified memory truth or human reviews. Provenance combines exact construction relationships with controlled synthetic contributors and AI span-backed claim origins. Principal records stay separate; unresolved origins cannot propagate. Case labels never become memory findings, and target judges never update trust.

The original first audit ticket is preserved even when its AI assessment is safe or unresolved. feedback_by_cell in comparison.json reports all ticket outcomes. These runs therefore do not implement the primary protocol's study conditional on an independently confirmed unsafe seed.

Source-grounded benign QA remains unavailable. Original retention/restraint alone cannot establish preservation of useful external contributions. Refusals are not independently annotated; nonempty generated outputs do not prove usefulness. Empty outputs, token-limit finishes, fallbacks and actual source-policy selection changes are logged in diagnostics.

Audit roots are excluded from query cohorts, but foreign root records can still be retrieved. An answer difference does not by itself identify transfer to uninspected siblings. All R/C/M/S methods receive the same B=5 feedback and equivalent verifier resources within each regime; static/no-memory references consume no audits.
